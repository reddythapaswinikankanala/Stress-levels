STRESS LEVELS AND COPING MECHANISMS

A Community Service Project focused on understanding stress and promoting healthy coping methods in society.

About Our Project

Stress is a common experience in everyday life. Students and community members may experience stress due to studies, work, family responsibilities and other daily challenges.

This project aims to create awareness about stress levels, understand its causes and encourage healthy coping mechanisms for better well-being.

Our Main Objectives

  • Understand different stress levels.
  • Identify common causes of stress.
  • Study the effects of stress on daily life.
  • Promote healthy coping mechanisms.
  • Create awareness among students and society.
  • Encourage community participation.

Project Activities

Eight-week Community Service Project documentation.

Week 1 – Planning and Questionnaire Design

Project planning, community selection and preparation of survey questions.

Week 2 – Community Survey

Collect information about stress awareness and common challenges.

Week 3 – Awareness Campaign

Share information about stress and healthy coping methods.

Week 4 – Deep-Dive Survey

Study community responses and identify common stress factors.

Week 5 – Community Intervention

Conduct awareness activities and encourage healthy habits.

Week 6 – Impact Assessment

Collect feedback and understand changes in awareness.

Week 7 – Report Preparation

Organize survey information, photographs and observations.

Week 8 – Final Documentation

Prepare and submit the final CSP report.
