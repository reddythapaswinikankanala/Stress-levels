Welcome to Our Project

Stress is a common part of everyday life. Understanding stress and learning healthy coping methods can improve our well-being.

About the Project

This Community Service Project creates awareness about stress among students and society. It explains common causes, stress levels and healthy ways to manage stress.

Different Stress Levels

Low Stress

Feeling calm and comfortable.

Moderate Stress

Experiencing some pressure or tension.

High Stress

Feeling overwhelmed and needing support.

Coping Mechanisms

  • Practice relaxation and comfortable breathing.
  • Maintain a regular sleep routine.
  • Plan daily activities.
  • Talk with trusted people.
  • Enjoy hobbies and take breaks.
  • Seek professional support when needed.
